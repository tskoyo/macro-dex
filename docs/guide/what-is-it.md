# What is Macro DEX?

Macro DEX is a simple place to sell one token for another at a price you choose.

Think of a real farmers market. A farmer brings a crate of apples and puts a price tag on it. A shopper walks by, likes the price, and buys the whole crate. That's it.

Here it works the same way, with tokens instead of apples.

## The two roles

**Farmers** sell. A farmer puts up some tokens (for example, ETH) and sets a price (for example, 2,500 DAI per ETH). Their tokens are held by the market until someone buys them or the farmer takes them back.

**Buyers** shop. A buyer looks at the open orders, picks one, and buys all of it in a single step. The payment goes straight to the farmer.

## What makes it different

- **You always buy the whole order.** No partial fills. What you see is exactly what you get.
- **The price never moves.** The price tag is set when the order is placed and can't change.
- **No middleman.** There's no company running it, no fee taken, and no one who can pause it or change the rules.
- **Orders can't vanish on you.** A farmer chooses a waiting period of 1 to 5 days. Until it's over, the order stays up and anyone can buy it.

## Which tokens?

Each market trades one pair. One token is being sold (we call it the **base** token, such as ETH or BTC). The other is used to pay (the **quote** token, such as DAI or USDC).

A separate market is launched for each pair, for example ETH/DAI or BTC/USDC.
